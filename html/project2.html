My Climbing Update

Climbing has become one of my favorite hobbies. It helps with reconnecting with the outdoors and keeping me in shape while I work and attend school from home. I started climbing indoors around the end of the pandemic in 2021, and within my first year I was climbing V4 to V5 and steadily improving. Since then, I have worked my way up to V8 to V9 indoors and have flashed a few climbs at that level.

Outdoor climbing has also become an important part of my life. My dad introduced me to climbing when I was younger, but I did not fully appreciate it until I gained more experience in the gym. I enjoy both indoor and outdoor climbing because they challenge me in different ways. Unfortunately, I do not find as much time to climb outside as I would like.

Indoor Climbing Grades

  • Bouldering: V8–V9
  • Auto Belay: 5.12
  • Lead: 5.10+

Outdoor Climbing Grades

  • Bouldering: V5-V6
  • Top Rope: 5.12
  • Lead: 5.11+
